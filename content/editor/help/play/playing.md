# Playing

Press **Play** at the top, or `F5`. The level is saved, [built](play/building) if it needs to be, and starts in the editor window.

- The arrow next to Play chooses where you start: the **player start**, or **from here** (where the camera is). `Shift`+click flips it for one go.
- `Esc` pauses and brings the editor back: see [Live edits](play/live-edits).
- `F5` carries on; `F10` stops and returns to the editor.
