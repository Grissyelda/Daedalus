# Changes to one copy

Double-click a part of a copy to select just that part. Whatever you change about it then (move it, paint it, change a light's colour) is that copy's own: the other copies don't change, and when the piece changes, this copy keeps its own version of what you changed. Deleting a part from a copy works the same way.

The Inspector marks these with a dot:
- **Revert** goes back to the piece's version.
- **Apply to all copies** puts the change into the piece, so every copy gets it.

With the whole copy selected, the Piece card says how many things this copy changes, with **Revert all** and **Apply to all copies** for all of them at once.
