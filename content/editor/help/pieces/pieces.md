# Pieces

A piece is something you build once and use again and again: a lamp post, a doorway, a house. Every copy stays linked to its piece, so changing the piece changes them all.

### Making one
Select what goes in it and press **Make piece** (in the Kits tab, or the bar above the dock). Give it a name. What you selected becomes the first copy.

### Placing copies
Your pieces are tiles in the **Kits** tab: click one, or drag it into the view.

### Working with copies
- A click selects a whole copy; move and turn it like anything else.
- Double-click picks one part of it. Changing that part changes only this copy: see [Changes to one copy](pieces/overrides).
- **Edit piece** in the Inspector changes the piece itself: see [Editing a piece](pieces/editing-pieces).
- **Open on its own** opens the piece by itself, like a small level.
- **Unlink** turns a copy into ordinary things, no longer linked.

Pieces can hold other pieces: a house made of window pieces changes when the window does.
