# Doors

A door is a brush that slides. Draw or place the brush the size of the door, select it, and in the **Logic** tab pick the door under **Turn into**.

In the [Inspector](panels/inspector), the **Door** card sets:
- **Direction**: where it slides to. The violet arrow in the view shows it.
- **Wait**: how long it stays open.
- **Start open** and **Locked**.

It opens when the player comes near, unless it's locked. **Undo it** in the Logic tab turns it back into plain brushes.
