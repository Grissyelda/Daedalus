# Lights

The **Light** tab has lights ready to go: **Small**, **Room**, **Big**, **Warm** and **Cool**. Click one to add it in the middle of the view, or drag it where you want it.

In the [Inspector](panels/inspector), the **Light** card has:
- **Colour**: click the swatch to change it.
- **Reach**: how far it lights, in each direction.
- **Shadows**: whether things in its light cast shadows.

> Lights changed while the game is paused change in the game straight away. See [Live edits](play/live-edits).
