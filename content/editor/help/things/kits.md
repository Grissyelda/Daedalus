# Kits

The **Kits** tab has every kind of thing your project has, ready to place, plus your own [pieces](pieces/pieces). Click a tile to add one in the middle of the view, or drag it where you want it. Hover a tile to see what it is.

At the end of the row are the [models](things/models) you have brought in, and **Bring in** for a new one.
