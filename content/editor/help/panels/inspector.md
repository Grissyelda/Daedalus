# Inspector

Shows what's selected and lets you change it, as cards. Each card's **?** explains it; its arrow folds it away.

- **Placement**: where it is and how big, or which way it faces.
- **Shape** for brushes, **Material** and **Texture** for surfaces.
- **Light**, **Door** and other cards for things.
- **Piece** for copies of [pieces](pieces/pieces).
- **Advanced**: every setting as the raw keys the game reads.

With nothing selected, **Level settings** shows the settings for the whole level.
