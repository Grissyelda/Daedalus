# Keys

### Camera
- `Right mouse` look, with `W` `A` `S` `D` `Q` `E` to fly, `Shift` faster
- `Middle mouse` pan, `Alt` + `Left mouse` orbit, `Wheel` forward and back
- `F` frame the selection, `T` top view

### Tools
- `W` [Move](tools/move), `E` [Turn](tools/turn), `R` [Resize](tools/resize)
- `B` [Draw](tools/draw), `C` [Cut](tools/cut), `V` [Corners](tools/corners), `P` [Paint](tools/paint), `I` pick up a material
- `Space` (hold) the [tool wheel](tools/tool-wheel)
- `1` to `5` the dock's tabs: Build, Paint, Logic, Light, Kits
- `[` and `]` grid smaller and bigger, `,` and `.` turn by 90 degrees

### Editing
- `Ctrl+Z` undo, `Ctrl+Y` redo, `Ctrl+S` save, `Ctrl+D` duplicate, `Delete` delete
- `Ctrl+B` [build](play/building)

### Playing
- `F5` play (or carry on), `Esc` pause, `F10` stop

### Help
- `F1` opens and closes this panel
