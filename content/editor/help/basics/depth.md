# Create, Understand, Control

Every part of Daedalus has three levels of depth. The switch at the top of the window, next to the menus, picks how much the editor shows.

- **Create** shows just what you need to make things: shapes, paint, lights, Play. Deeper settings appear as a quiet **More:** line at the bottom of a panel. Click it to open that card here without changing the whole editor.
- **Understand** also shows the settings behind each thing, folded: the **Advanced** card with every raw setting a thing has, a material's **Behaviour**, and so on.
- **Control** opens everything, including the files Daedalus writes for you (a material's **File** card shows its material text).

Nothing you make depends on the depth: it only changes what you see. Switch up when you want to know how something works, and back down when you just want to build.
