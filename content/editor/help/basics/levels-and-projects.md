# Levels and projects

A **project** is your game: its levels, materials, models and settings live in its own folder. A **level** is one map in it.

- **File > New Map** starts a level with a floor, a light and a player start. **File > Save As** saves a copy under a new name.
- Levels are listed in the [Content](panels/content) panel; double-click one to open it.
- **File > New Project** makes a project from the template and asks whether it starts with the stylized or the realistic (PBR) look. **File > Open Project** switches to another; the editor restarts into it.
- The project's name is at the top right.
