# Moving around

The view is where you build. Moving the camera never changes the level.

### In the 3D view
- **Look:** hold the right mouse button and move the mouse.
- **Fly:** while holding the right mouse button, `W` `A` `S` `D` move, `Q` and `E` go down and up. Hold `Shift` to go faster; the wheel changes the speed.
- **Pan:** hold the middle mouse button and drag.
- **Orbit:** hold `Alt` and drag with the left mouse button to circle around a point.
- **Move forward and back:** roll the wheel.
- **Frame the selection:** press `F` to fly to what's selected.

### From above
`T` switches to the [top view](tools/top-view), a plan of the level from above, and back.

> Lost? Select something in the [Outliner](panels/outliner) and press `F`.
