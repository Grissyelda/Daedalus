# Selecting

Most tools work on what's selected. What's selected shows in the [Inspector](panels/inspector).

- **Click** something to select it.
- `Ctrl`**+click** adds to the selection, or takes something back out.
- `Shift`**+click** picks a single face of a brush (to paint it, or line up its texture).
- **Click empty space** to select nothing.
- In the [Outliner](panels/outliner), click a row; double-click to fly to it.

A click on a copy of a [piece](pieces/pieces) selects the whole copy. Double-click to pick one part of it.

The bar above the dock shows what you can do with the selection: turn it by 90 degrees, carve with it, make a piece, duplicate (`Ctrl+D`), frame it (`F`) or delete it (`Delete`).
