# Your first piece

Pieces are things you build once and place again and again: a lamp post, a doorway, a whole house. Every copy stays linked to the piece. See [Pieces](pieces/pieces) for the full story.

- [ ] **Select something to reuse.** Click a brush, `Ctrl`+click to add more (a light too, if you like). {check: select.any}
- [ ] **Make a piece.** Press **Make piece** in the Kits tab (or in the bar above the dock) and give it a name. {check: piece.made} {show: kits/makepiece}
- [ ] **Place a copy.** In the **Kits** tab, click your piece's tile or drag it into the view. {check: piece.placed} {show: tab:Kits}
- [ ] **Change one copy.** Double-click one part of a copy and change it: move it, or paint it. The Inspector marks it as this copy's own. {check: piece.override}
- [ ] **Edit the piece.** Select a copy and press **Edit piece** in the Inspector. The rest of the level turns to ghosts. {check: piece.edit}
- [ ] **Save it to every copy.** Change something, then press **Save to every copy** above the view. {check: piece.saved.all}

> Changed your mind? **Undo** (`Ctrl+Z`) takes back a saved piece as well.
