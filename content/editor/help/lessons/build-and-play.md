# Build, play and change things as you go

The game plays a built version of your level. Building runs in the background, so you keep working while it does.

- [ ] **Build the level.** Press `Ctrl+B`, or the hammer left of Play. The bar shows how far it has got. {check: build.done} {show: build}
- [ ] **Play from here.** Fly the camera somewhere, open the arrow next to Play and pick **Play from here (the camera)**, then press Play. {check: play.start} {show: play}
- [ ] **Pause.** Press `Esc`. The game stays where it is and the editor comes back. {check: play.pause}
- [ ] **Add a light while paused.** It appears in the paused game straight away. {check: did:Add light} {show: light/Room}
- [ ] **Carry on.** Press `F5`. If you changed brushes, the level rebuilds and you carry on where you were. {check: play.resume}
- [ ] **Stop.** Press `F10` to end the play session. {check: play.stop}

> If the build says the level **leaks**, see [Building](play/building).
