# Getting started

A first level in a few minutes. Each step ticks itself off when you do it, in any order. **Show me** makes the right button glow.

- [ ] **Look around.** Hold the right mouse button over the view and move the mouse. {check: camera.look}
- [ ] **Fly.** Keep holding the right mouse button and press `W` `A` `S` `D` to move, `Q` and `E` to go down and up. {check: camera.fly}
- [ ] **Add a room.** In the Build tab of the dock, drag the **Room** tile into the view. {check: shape.added} {show: build/Room}
- [ ] **Move it.** Click it to select it, pick **Move** (`W`) and drag one of the arrows. {check: did:Move} {show: build/Move}
- [ ] **Turn it.** Pick **Turn** (`E`) and drag a ring, or press `,` or `.` to turn by 90 degrees. {check: did:Turn} {show: build/Turn}
- [ ] **Paint it.** Open the **Paint** tab and click a wall. {check: did:Paint} {show: tab:Paint}
- [ ] **Light it.** In the **Light** tab, drag the **Room** light into the room. {check: did:Add light} {show: light/Room}
- [ ] **Play.** Press **Play** at the top, or `F5`. {check: play.start} {show: play}
- [ ] **Pause and carry on.** Press `Esc` to pause, change something, then `F5` to carry on. {check: play.resume}

> Every step has a page of its own: see [Moving around](basics/moving-around), [Shapes](tools/shapes), [Move](tools/move) and [Playing](play/playing).
