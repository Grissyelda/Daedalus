# Move

`W`. Select something and drag an arrow to slide it along that direction, or a square between two arrows to slide it across that plane. It snaps to the grid.

- The grid size is in the view bar (top left); `[` and `]` change it. The magnet turns snapping off.
- In the [Inspector](panels/inspector), the **Placement** card lets you type a position.
- In the [top view](tools/top-view) you can just drag things.

> Textures stay put on surfaces as brushes move while **Tex lock** (Paint tab) is on.
