# Cut

`C`. Splits brushes along a line you click:

1. Select the brushes to cut.
2. Click two points on them (a third tilts the cut).
3. Choose which side stays (`Tab` cycles: both, yellow or blue) and press `Enter` or **Cut**.

`Esc` starts over. For holes, like doorways and windows, [Carve](tools/carve) is quicker.
