# Paint

`P`, or the **Paint** tab. Click surfaces to put the current material on them.

- `Ctrl`+click paints the whole brush.
- **Pick** (`I`), then click a surface, takes its material to paint with.
- The material tile shows what you're painting with; click it to choose another.
- **Apply** paints everything selected; **Fit** stretches the texture to cover each selected face once.
- **Tex lock** keeps textures in place on surfaces when brushes move or turn.

To line up one face's texture, `Shift`+click it and use the **Texture** card in the [Inspector](panels/inspector).
