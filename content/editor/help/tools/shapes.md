# Shapes

The Build tab has ready-made shapes: **Box**, **Room** (a hollow box with walls, floor and ceiling), **Ramp**, **Cylinder**, **Cone**, **Stairs** and **Arch**.

- **Click** a shape tile, then drag in the view to [draw](tools/draw) one any size.
- **Drag** a shape tile into the view to drop one at its usual size where you let go.

Shapes are made of brushes like everything else, so [Move](tools/move), [Resize](tools/resize), [Cut](tools/cut) and [Paint](tools/paint) work on them.
