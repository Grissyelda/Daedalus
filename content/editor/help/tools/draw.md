# Draw

`B`. Draws a new brush any size:

1. Drag a footprint on the floor and let go.
2. Move the mouse up or down for the height.
3. Click.

`Esc` cancels. The bar above the dock has the height and the floor it draws on. The dock's shape tiles (Box, Room, Ramp...) choose what gets drawn; see [Shapes](tools/shapes).

> In the [top view](tools/top-view), drag on empty space to draw.
